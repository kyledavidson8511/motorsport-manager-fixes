# Motorsport Manager Fixes & QoL 1.2.0

First public release.

- Fix: a driver's car follows his race seat, so Part Fitting and driver morale stop using the wrong car after driver swaps.
- Fix: both drivers' car opinions are recalculated when Part Fitting opens and whenever either car's parts change.
- New: left/right arrows (and arrow keys) step through the scouting list from a driver, designer or mechanic detail screen.
- New: a sortable Marketability column replaces Nationality in Scouting when listing drivers.
